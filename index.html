VyapaarBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

The short version. Your books — customers, bills, stock, cash — are kept in a private file on your own phone. We do not run a server, we have no copy of your data, and there is no account to create. If you choose to switch on Google Drive backup, the backup goes to your Google Drive, in your own Google account. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the VyapaarBook Android application (com.vyapaarbook.app), referred to below as "the app".

What you put in, and where it is kept

You enter your own business records: customers and suppliers, khata entries, invoices, quotations, bookings, purchases, inventory items and cash entries. All of it is written to the app's private storage area on your device, which no other app can read.

There is no sign-up, no OTP and no user account. The app works completely offline and never needs an internet connection to record a sale.

We receive nothing

We operate no server for this app. Your business data is never transmitted to us, and we have no ability to see it, recover it or share it. There is no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no third-party tracking in the application.

Google Drive backup — optional, and to your own account

If you choose to sign in with Google inside the app, two things happen:

  • Google tells the app your email address. It is stored on your device only, so the app can show you which account backups go to. It is not transmitted to us.
  • The app can then upload a backup file of your books to your own Google Drive.

The app uses the drive.file permission scope. This is deliberately the narrowest option available: it allows the app to see and manage only the files it has itself created. The app cannot see, read or list anything else in your Google Drive.

Backups stored in your Drive are covered by Google's own terms and privacy policy, and are under your control. You can delete them from Drive at any time. You can sign out inside the app, which stops all backup activity.

Signing in is entirely optional. The app is fully usable without it.

Android's own backup

This app allows Android's standard app-backup feature, which may copy the app's data to your Google account as part of your device backup. This is a function of the Android operating system, is tied to your own Google account, and can be turned off in your phone's Settings under Google → Backup. We receive nothing from it.

Camera, photos and contacts

  • Camera — used only when you scan a barcode or take a photo of an inventory item. Photos you take are stored inside the app's private storage on the device. No image is uploaded anywhere.
  • Gallery — when you pick an existing photo, Android's system picker hands the app that one image. The app holds no permission to browse your photo library.
  • Contacts — when you fill a customer from your contacts, Android's system picker returns only the one contact you select. The app holds no permission to read your contact list.

Payments

If you buy a paid plan, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details at any point.

What we would ever receive

Only what you deliberately send us. If you use "Email Support" or send the in-app problem log, the message and anything you attach arrives in our support mailbox. We use it to answer you and nothing else.

Sharing and selling

Your business data is never shared with anyone, never sold and never transferred to any third party. There is no mechanism in the app by which this could occur.

Your control

  • Download a full backup file at any time, to a location you choose.
  • Export your data as CSV or Excel at any time.
  • Delete any individual record inside the app.
  • "Erase ALL data" in Settings permanently removes everything stored on the device.
  • Sign out of Google inside the app to stop all Drive backup.
  • Delete backup files from your own Google Drive whenever you wish.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

Children

The app is a business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
